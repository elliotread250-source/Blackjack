How to play

Take turns dropping a disc into one of the seven columns. It falls to the lowest empty space. The first player to line up four of their discs in a row, across, up and down or diagonally, wins. If the board fills up with no four in a row, it's a draw.

Controls

  • Mouse: point at a column to preview your disc, click to drop it.
  • Touch: tap a column to drop. Touch and slide to preview first, then let go.
  • Keyboard: 1–7 drop straight into a column, or ← → to choose and Enter / Space / ↓ to drop.
  • U undo, N new game, M sound, Esc menu.

Modes

  • Vs Bot: Easy plays mostly at random. Medium looks a few moves ahead. Hard searches 8 moves deep and knows about threats. Impossible uses a time-limited deep search. It solves positions exactly when the remaining moves fit within its search budget; it is not guaranteed unbeatable. Choose who moves first; the player who starts has the advantage.
  • Pass & Play: two players on one device. Red and Yellow take turns starting.
  • Online: create a room and send the code or link to a friend, join with a code, or use quick match. The server referees every move. If your connection drops you have a few seconds to come back.

Undo works against the bot and in pass & play. Scores are kept for the session.

Tips

  • The middle column is part of the most fours. Start there.
  • Make two threats at once: your opponent can only block one.
  • Watch the spaces just above your opponent's discs: never fill the space below their winning spot.
