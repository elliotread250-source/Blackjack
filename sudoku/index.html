How to play

Fill the grid so every row, every column and every 3×3 box holds the digits 1 to 9 exactly once. Every puzzle here has a single solution that can be reached by logic alone, no guessing.

Select a cell, then pick a number. Turn on Notes to pencil in candidates instead. Hint fills in one cell and tells you the technique that finds it.

Levels

Easy needs only singles. Medium needs pointing/claiming or pairs and triples. Hard needs X-Wings, Skyscrapers, XY-Wings or quads. Expert needs Swordfish, colouring, W-Wings or XY-Chains. The daily puzzle is the same for everyone and gets harder through the week.
