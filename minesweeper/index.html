How to Play

Open every square that is not a mine. A number tells you how many of the eight squares around it hide mines. The first click is always safe.

Mouse

  • Left click opens a square.
  • Right click plants a flag (and a ? with Marks on).
  • Click a number whose flags are all placed, or middle click, or press both buttons, to open all its other neighbours.

Touch

  • Tap opens, long-press flags, tap a number to open around it.
  • Flag mode makes a tap plant flags instead; long-press then opens.
  • Use − / + to zoom big boards.

Keys

F2 or N new game · 1 2 3 Beginner / Intermediate / Expert · M sound · + − 0 zoom
