How To Play

Guess the Wordle in 6 tries.

  • Each guess must be a valid 5-letter word.
  • The color of the tiles will change to show how close your guess was to the word.

Examples

WORDY

W is in the word and in the correct spot.

LIGHT

I is in the word but in the wrong spot.

ROGUE

U is not in the word in any spot.

Letters can repeat. If you guess a letter twice and the word has it once, only one tile lights up (green first, then the leftmost).

Daily: one word a day, the same for everyone, new at midnight your time. Unlimited: a fresh random word whenever you like, with its own statistics.

Hard Mode (in settings): any revealed hints must be used in later guesses.

Type on your keyboard or tap the keys. Enter submits, Backspace deletes, Esc closes this window.
