# Cookie Clicker

Click the big cookie. Spend cookies on twelve buildings — Cursor, Grandma, Farm, Mine, Factory, Bank,
Temple, Wizard Tower, Shipment, Alchemy Lab, Portal and Time Machine — whose prices rise 15% with each
one you own.

- 58 upgrades: four doubling tiers per building, mouse upgrades that add 1% of your CpS to every click,
  and cookie flavours that raise all production.
- Golden cookies drift onto the screen every few minutes: **Lucky** (a lump of cookies), **Frenzy**
  (×7 production for 77 s) or **Click frenzy** (×777 clicks for 13 s).
- 30 achievements, each worth +1% production; a news ticker that follows your progress; stats.
- Buy 1, 10 or 100 at a time. Autosaves every 10 seconds, and keeps baking while you're away (up to a day).

`node tools/test.mjs` checks prices, production, upgrades, golden cookies, offline baking, saves and
a simulated six-hour game.
