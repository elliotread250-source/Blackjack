Guide Pac-Man through the maze and eat every dot to clear the level while four ghosts chase you. Power pellets in the corners turn the ghosts blue for a short time so you can eat them. Arrow keys or W A S D steer, Enter or Space starts, P pauses, M mutes. Use the How to play button for the full instructions.

HOW TO PLAY

Eat every dot in the maze to clear the level. Four ghosts hunt you; touching one costs a life.

Power pellets

The four large pellets turn the ghosts blue for a few seconds. Eat blue ghosts for 200, 400, 800 and 1,600 points. Their eyes fly home and they come back.

Controls

  • ← ↑ → ↓ or W A S D — steer (a turn is held until it fits)
  • Enter or Space — start / resume
  • P or Esc — pause · M — sound · H — this help
  • Touch: tap to start, swipe anywhere to steer, or use the d-pad
  • Gamepad: d-pad or stick, A to start, Start to pause

Scoring

Dot 10 · power pellet 50 · fruit 100–5,000 · extra life at 10,000. The game pauses by itself if you switch tabs or windows.
