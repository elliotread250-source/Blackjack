    How to play

    Your neighbour's basement door is padlocked, chained and glowing red. Get inside his house and find a way into the basement. He patrols his house and yard on a routine: kitchen, TV, garden, upstairs. If he sees or hears you he comes to look; if he spots you he gives chase. Get caught and you wake up back home, and whatever you carried is lying where he grabbed you.

    He learns

    • Bear traps where he keeps catching you (step in one and you're stuck, and he hears it).
    • Security cameras where he keeps spotting you (they call him over).
    • Boards or a chain on the way in you use most. A crowbar deals with both.

    Staying hidden

    • Crouch to be quiet and harder to see, especially in the dark. Sprinting is loud.
    • Throw things to distract him: he goes to check the noise.
    • Hide in wardrobes and cupboards — unless he saw you get in.
    • The eye at the top shows how much he has noticed you.

    Desktop

    WASD move · Shift sprint · C / Ctrl crouch · Space jump · E use / open / pick up · Left mouse pick up, or hold to charge a throw · Right mouse / Q drop · 1–4 / wheel pick a slot · F hide / come out · R push (once you have the power) · Esc pause. Look at a crate while holding another and drop it to stack them; jump onto stacks to climb.

    Phone / tablet

    Hold the phone sideways. Left thumb: move (push to the edge to sprint). Right side: drag to look. Use opens, picks up and hides; Throw (hold to charge); Jump, Crouch, Drop; tap the slots to switch items.
