English pool (blackball)

Seven reds, seven yellows and the black. Break from anywhere behind the baulk line (the line near the bottom/left of the table). A legal break needs at least two balls to reach a cushion or a ball potted. If the black goes down on the break, the balls are re-racked and the same player breaks again.

The table is open after the break: the first player to legally pot a red or a yellow takes that colour. Keep shooting while you pot your own balls. Clear all seven of your colour, then pot the black to win the frame.

Fouls

  • In-off: the cue ball goes in a pocket (or leaves the table).
  • Missing every ball, or hitting your opponent's ball or the black first (you may only hit the black first once you are on it).
  • Potting one of your opponent's balls.
  • No ball reaching a cushion after contact when nothing is potted.

You lose the frame if you pot the black before your colour is cleared, or pot the black and foul on the same shot (for example an in-off on the black). With "Call the black's pocket" on, the black must go in the pocket you chose.

Pub rules (default)

  • After a foul your opponent gets two shots (two visits). The first is a free shot: they may hit any ball first without fouling.
  • Potting one of your own balls keeps you at the table and you still have the remaining visit.
  • Two shots are never carried onto the black: a player on the black gets one visit.
  • After an in-off the cue ball is in hand behind the baulk line and must be played out of baulk (not at a ball in baulk first, unless it is the free shot).

Blackball (WPA/EPA)

  • After any foul your opponent gets the cue ball in hand anywhere on the table and one visit. No two shots.

Controls

  • Aim: move the mouse over the table, or drag anywhere on the table with a finger (tap a ball to aim straight at it). Hold Shift for fine mouse aim; arrow keys or the fine-aim wheel nudge the aim.
  • Shoot: press on the table and pull the mouse back, then release. On touch, drag the power bar and let go. Space shoots at the power set with Up/Down.
  • Spin: tap the cue-ball button and place the tip: top for follow, bottom for draw, left/right for side.
  • Ball in hand: drag the cue ball to a free spot.
  • Call pocket: when on the black with calling on, click/tap a pocket.
  • M mutes, Esc opens the menu.
