How to play

  • Drag across touching letters (sideways, up, down or diagonal) to spell a word, then let go. Or tap letters one by one and tap the last one again (or Submit).
  • On a keyboard, just type the word and press Enter. The path lights up as you type.
  • Each cube once per word. Words need 3+ letters (4+ in Big Boggle). Qu is one cube, two letters.
  • Scoring: 3–4 letters 1 · 5 2 · 6 3 · 7 5 · 8+ 11. Against the CPU, words you both find are crossed out.
