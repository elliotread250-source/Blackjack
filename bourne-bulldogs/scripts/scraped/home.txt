Welcome to Bourne Basketball Club!
Located in Bourne, Lincolnshire, our Basketball England registered club is dedicated to delivering first-class basketball training for youth, ages 8 to 16. Whether your child is a complete beginner or an experienced player, we offer an inclusive and supportive environment to nurture new skills, improve fitness, and learn to work as a team in a fun community environment.
Led by Martin Read, a former national league player & Great Britain Ladies’ coach, our training focuses on skills & game led scenarios which are delivered as fun competitive 1.5 hour sessions. Since opening our doors we’ve seen tremendous growth in our players, who have developed as individuals (skills & confidence) and as teammates & new friends!
At Bourne Bulldogs, we emphasise fitness, skill development and fun, offering a local safe space for our young athletes to thrive through competition and camaraderie within our community.
We welcome you to join our basketball family and be part of a vibrant community that’s passionate about the game.
